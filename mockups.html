03 / Broadsheet

The Threshold Gazette

Established this morning · Published once · Read carefully
Vol. I — No. I An Inquiry into Coordination One Vote, One Voice

A Question of Buttons

In which the reader is asked to imagine a vote upon which all life depends, and to record their honest answer.

Everyone in the world has to take a private vote by pressing a red or blue button. If more than 50% of people press the blue button, everyone survives. If the blue button does not clear that threshold, only those who pressed the red button survive. The vote is anonymous; no one will know what you chose; the only consequence is the consequence itself. Consider the question carefully, and then commit.
Which button would you, personally, press?
